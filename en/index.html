Every workout counts.

Football, running or yoga, it's all the same: every workout you do or plan goes into that day's calorie target. And you get a heads-up before each one, so nothing slips your mind.

AI insights

Straight to the point: what's affecting you and what to do now.

No need to crunch the numbers yourself. In a few lines, the AI tells you what's affecting your form most and what to focus on over the next 1–3 days. “How your week is going” in the Calendar sums up your week the same way.

  • It comments on your form and explains why your plan looks the way it does. Type in a food that isn't in the database and it estimates the values, clearly marked as an estimate.
  • Your phone does the math, the AI explains it. It doesn't make up numbers.
  • When it isn't sure, it says so. No bluffing.
  • It never makes up sources and never oversells the evidence.
  • Everything it writes is safety-checked before it reaches you. If something health-related comes up, it points you to a doctor.
  • Your name and email never reach it. Only if you say yes, it works from a short summary with your identity stripped out.

Quick setup

Eight quick steps. No sign-up.

Everything you enter during setup feeds into your target. At the end, the summary shows exactly how it was worked out.

  1. BasicsYour birth year and biological sex. The app is for people 18 and up.
  2. Privacy and permissionsAccept the terms, then decide separately whether to allow health data and AI, or skip both. You can change any of it later in Profile.
  3. BodyYour height and weight, in kg/cm or lb/ft-in.
  4. GoalLose fat, maintain, build muscle, perform better or just stay healthy. No crash diets: weight loss tops out at 1% a week.
  5. JobWhich days and hours do you work? Sitting at a desk, working in the field or on a building site changes how much energy you use.
  6. TrainingWhich sports do you play, how often and for how long? Your weekly plan is built from this.
  7. Food and healthFoods you don't eat, plus an optional quick health check.
  8. SummaryYour daily target and how it was worked out, line by line.

Frequently asked

The short version.

When does it launch?

FitWinner is in closed beta right now. You'll hear it here first when it lands on the App Store.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

Nope. Calorie tracking, food logging and your training plan all work without one. Connect Apple Watch or Apple Health and your resting heart rate, HRV and sleep feed into your form score.

What exactly does the AI do?

Only if you allow it, it adds short notes on your form score and plan, and estimates foods that aren't in the database. It doesn't do the math: your calorie target, macros and form score come from calculations on your phone.

What data does the AI get?

Not your name, email or account info (there's no account anyway). If you say yes, it gets a short, anonymous summary of what it needs, like workout type and length and your recent form scores. Take your permission back and the comments made from that data are deleted too.

Does the AI cost extra?

On-phone comments are free. Detailed AI comments, plan explanations and food estimates are part of Premium, and all of it is optional.

Is it medical advice?

No. FitWinner is for people 18 and up. It's not a medical device and it doesn't diagnose or treat anything. If you have a health condition, talk to your doctor about your goals.

What languages is it in?

The app is available in 11 languages: Turkish, English, French, Dutch, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Greek, Czech and Estonian. This site is in Turkish and English for now.
