From football to yoga

Every workout you do is added to that day's calorie target.

Whether it's football, running, yoga or a session at the gym, every workout you complete or add to your calendar updates that day's calorie target. On training days you see the energy your body needs, and on rest days a more balanced target. Once notifications are on, the app sends these reminders around your training time: a carb-focused, low-fat meal suggestion about two hours before the session; a reminder of the day's plan as early as you chose; fluid and electrolyte reminders for sessions longer than 60 minutes; a recovery meal with protein and carbs afterwards; and a carb-loading suggestion the evening before a match. The gram amounts in these notifications are calculated from your weight.

Workout → calorie target

The energy a workout uses is calculated from the type of sport, how long it lasted and how hard you pushed yourself. If your Apple Watch measured your heart rate during the session, that measurement is used. Active calories your watch has already recorded are never added a second time, so the same activity doesn't count toward your target twice.

A timely reminder for every workout

Once you turn on workout reminders in the Calendar, you can choose whether they arrive 30, 60, 90 or 120 minutes before a workout. The workout time comes from the time you entered for that day when adding it to the calendar, or from your general training time if you didn't. Nutrition reminders are set around the same time: a light meal about two hours before training, fluids at the start of workouts longer than 60 minutes, a recovery meal half an hour after you finish and a carb-loading reminder the evening before a match. You can choose to receive electrolyte reminders in the summer months, always or never. Skipped workouts don't get notifications, and when your plan changes the reminders are rearranged automatically.

AI insights

It explains what's affecting you and your next step, in plain language.

Your form score is built from several components, such as sleep, fatigue, muscle soreness, stress and training load, and these numbers aren't always easy to interpret one by one. The AI identifies what is affecting your form most and explains, in a few sentences, what you might focus on over the next one to three days. “How your week is going” in the Calendar summarises your weekly plan in the same way.

  • It interprets your form and explains why your weekly plan is structured the way it is. When you enter a food that isn't in the database, it estimates the nutritional values and the app clearly marks them as estimates.
  • All calculations are done by the calculation engines on your phone; the AI only interprets the results and never produces new numbers of its own.
  • When the available information isn't enough to reach a conclusion, it says so clearly, and the app also shows you that the comment is uncertain.
  • It doesn't invent sources or name specific studies, and it never presents information as more certain than the scientific evidence allows.
  • Everything it writes passes an automatic safety review before it reaches you. If an illness, medication or clinical symptom comes up, it doesn't give advice and instead suggests speaking with a doctor.
  • Your name, email address and any other information that could identify you are never shared with the AI. It works only with your permission, from a short, anonymous summary containing what it needs for the comment.

03

Recovery and form score

Each day, a short check-in lets you note your sleep duration and quality, fatigue, muscle soreness and stress; if your Apple Watch is connected, your resting heart rate and HRV are taken into account too. Today's values are compared with your own values from the last 28 days rather than with other people's, the balance of your training load over the past week is added with a smaller weight, and the result becomes a form score between 0 and 100. Your first score appears after at least seven days of check-ins and is shown with lower confidence during the first two weeks. On days when the score is low, that day's workout is made lighter.

Quick setup

Eight short steps, no account required.

Every piece of information you share during setup is used to calculate your daily target, and none of it is tied to an account. In the final step, the summary shows line by line which information your target is based on and how it was put together.

  1. BasicsYour birth year and biological sex. The app is for people aged 18 and over.
  2. Privacy and permissionsYou accept the terms of use. You can grant or decline permission for health data and AI separately, and change these choices later in Profile.
  3. BodyYour height and weight, in kg/cm or lb/ft-in.
  4. GoalFat loss, maintaining your weight, building muscle, performance or general health. When losing weight, the weekly target is limited to at most 1% of your body weight.
  5. JobWhich days and how many hours you work. Working at a desk, in the field or on a building site changes your daily energy needs.
  6. TrainingThe sports you do, how often and for how long. Your weekly plan is built from this information.
  7. Food and healthFoods you don't eat and, if you wish, a short health screening.
  8. SummaryYour daily target and how it was worked out, line by line.

Health screening, free in every version

It never makes a diagnosis. Your answers are only used to make your goals safer; for example, no calorie deficit is applied during pregnancy. When appropriate, it refers you to a specialist.

Your privacy comes first

All calculations happen on your phone, so no internet connection is needed and your raw health data never leaves your phone.

No account required

You don't need to create an account or share your email address to use the app. The information you enter and your logs are stored on your phone; only with your permission is a short, anonymous summary sent for AI comments.

Consistent, transparent calculations

Your calorie target, macros and form score are determined by the calculation engines on your phone. Because these calculations follow the same rules every time, entering the same information always gives the same result, and you can see how that result was reached.

AI only with your permission

Comments are prepared only with your permission, from a summary with your name and identifying details removed. You can withdraw permission at any time, and comments made from that data are deleted too.

Frequently asked questions

Questions you may have.

When will FitWinner be available?

FitWinner is currently in closed beta. We'll announce it on this page when it becomes available on the App Store.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No, it isn't required. Calorie tracking, food logging and your training plan all work without one. If you connect Apple Watch or Apple Health, your resting heart rate, HRV and sleep are included in your form score.

What does the AI do?

Only with your permission, it interprets your form score and plan in short notes and estimates foods that aren't in the database. The AI doesn't perform the calculations; your calorie target, macros and form score come from calculations on your phone.

What information is shared with the AI?

Your name, email address and account details are never shared, and the app doesn't use accounts at all. With your permission, a short, anonymous summary is sent, such as workout type and duration and your recent form scores. If you withdraw permission, comments made from that data are deleted too.

Are the AI features paid?

Comments prepared on your phone are free. Detailed AI comments, plan explanations and food estimates are part of Premium, and all of them are optional.

Does it give medical advice?

No. FitWinner is for people aged 18 and over. It isn't a medical device; it doesn't diagnose or recommend treatment. If you have a health condition, we recommend discussing your goals with your doctor.

Which languages are available?

The app is available in 11 languages: Turkish, English, French, Dutch, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Greek, Czech and Estonian. This site is currently available in Turkish and English.
